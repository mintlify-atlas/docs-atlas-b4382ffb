Ratatui (_ˌræ.təˈtu.i_) is a Rust crate for cooking up terminal user interfaces. It gives you a
full widget toolkit, a constraint-based layout engine, and support for multiple terminal backends —
all in a single, ergonomic library that compiles to fast, lightweight binaries.

## What Makes Ratatui Different

<Steps>
  <Step title="Immediate-mode rendering">
    On every frame, your app renders the full UI into an in-memory buffer. Ratatui diffs it
    against the previous frame and writes only the changed cells to the terminal — giving you
    flicker-free, efficient updates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Constraint-based layouts">
    Describe your UI with constraints like `Length`, `Percentage`, `Fill`, `Min`, and `Max`.
    Ratatui solves the layout using a cassowary-style solver and hands you the resulting `Rect`
    areas to render into.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rich widget library">
    Ship with Block, Paragraph, List, Table, Chart, Canvas, BarChart, Gauge, Sparkline, Tabs,
    Scrollbar, and Calendar — all composable and stylable out of the box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Multiple backends">
    Ratatui works with Crossterm (Windows/macOS/Linux), Termion (Unix), Termwiz, and Termina.
    Swap backends without changing your application logic.
  </Step>
</Steps>
